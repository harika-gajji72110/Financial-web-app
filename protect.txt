You are a senior product designer, UX strategist, and solution architect for a modern personal finance and wealth management application.



Design a brand-new module called **“Protect”** for the application.



## Module Purpose

The purpose of the Protect module is to help users:

- Manage insurance policies

- Track financial protection coverage

- Store policy documents

- Monitor renewals and premium due dates

- Identify coverage gaps

- Improve overall financial protection readiness



The final output should feel like a **modern personal insurance management and financial protection platform fully integrated into a wealth management application**.



---



# IMPORTANT DESIGN GOALS

1. Create a premium, modern, intuitive user experience.

2. Make the system feel trustworthy, organized, and actionable.

3. Prioritize a mobile-first responsive design while also supporting desktop.

4. Ensure the module helps users understand whether they are financially protected.

5. Include visual indicators for active, expiring, due soon, renewed, and lapsed policies.

6. Include strong document management capability.

7. Include advanced but understandable protection insights and recommendations.

8. Include the ability to manage policies for self and family members if relevant.



---

# AGE-BASED INSIGHTS REQUIREMENT

Include **insights and recommendations based on the age or life stage of the person**, but do so thoughtfully and responsibly.



The module should generate age-aware protection guidance such as:

- Younger adults may need starter health cover, personal accident cover, and emergency protection

- Early family stage users may need stronger life cover, family health insurance, and child/family nominee planning

- Mid-life users may need higher health cover, critical illness cover, home/property protection, and long-term financial continuity planning

- Pre-retirement users may need hospitalization focus, asset protection, reduced liabilities, and legacy/nominee planning

- Senior users may need health continuity, hospitalization optimization, document accessibility, and dependent protection planning



Important:

- Use age-based insights as **financial education and personalization only**

- Do **not** make it discriminatory

- Treat age as a guidance parameter for coverage recommendations, not as an eligibility or exclusion mechanism

- If date of birth is unavailable, use user-provided age range or life-stage selection



Please clearly show:

1. Where age is captured or inferred

2. How age-based insights appear in the UI

3. How recommendations change by life stage

4. What disclaimers and privacy safeguards should exist

# BUSINESS & UX DETAILS TO CONSIDER

- A user has multiple policies from the same insurer

- A user manages insurance for spouse, children, or parents

- A policy can have more than one uploaded document

- Some policies may be active but expiring soon

- Some policies may lapse due to missed premiums

- Premium frequencies may differ (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly)

- Some policies may not have nominees yet
