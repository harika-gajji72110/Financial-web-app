You are a senior product designer, UX strategist, and solution architect for a modern personal finance and wealth management platform.

Design a new module called **“Loans & Liabilities”**.

---

# MODULE PURPOSE
The Loans & Liabilities module should help users:
- Track all outstanding loans and liabilities
- Monitor repayment progress
- Manage EMIs and due dates
- Analyze debt burden
- Record prepayments
- Understand how liabilities impact their overall net worth

The final product should feel like a **modern debt management and liability intelligence system integrated into a wealth management platform**.

---

# DESIGN GOALS
1. Provide clear visibility of debt at all times
2. Make repayment progress intuitive and motivating
3. Enable proactive EMI and repayment management
4. Help users understand financial health and risk
5. Integrate liabilities seamlessly with net worth calculations
6. Offer actionable insights and recommendations
7. Keep the interface clean, modern, and data-driven

---

# CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS

## 1) Liabilities Dashboard
Design a dashboard with summary cards showing:
- Total Outstanding Debt
- Total Monthly EMI
- Total Interest Payable
- Active Loans Count
- Debt-to-Income Ratio
- Loan Health Score

Add:
- Liability distribution by loan type (chart)
- Outstanding balance breakdown (chart)
- EMI trend analysis (chart)
- Alerts for overdue EMIs or high debt indicators

**Must include a prominent “Add Loan” button for quick entry.**

---

## 2) Loan Portfolio Management
Allow users to:
- Add loans
- View loans
- Edit loans
- Delete loans
- Search and filter loans

Supported categories:
- Home Loan
- Education Loan
- Vehicle Loan
- Personal Loan
- Credit Card Debt
- Gold Loan
- Business Loan
- Other Loan

For each loan collect:
- Loan Type
- Loan Provider / Bank
- Loan Account Number (Optional)
- **Borrower Name (Policy Holder equivalent for loans)**
- Original Loan Amount
- Outstanding Amount
- Interest Rate (%)
- EMI Amount
- Loan Start Date
- Loan End Date / Maturity Date
- Remaining Tenure (Months)
- Prepayment Amount (Optional)
- Notes

Suggest additional fields:
- Interest type (fixed/floating)
- Processing fee
- Co-applicant details
- Credit score impact
- Collateral type
- EMI auto-debit enabled
- Loan status (Active/Closed/Defaulted)

**Include “Add Loan” button here as well.**

## 3) Loan Details Screen

- Loan Information
- Borrower Name

- Interest Rate
- EMI details
- Remaining tenure
- Total interest payable
- Prepayment history

- Loan status (Active / Closed / Overdue)

Include actions:
- Record EMI payment

- Close loan
- Edit details

## 4) EMI Tracking
Design a dedicated EMI tracking section:
- Upcoming EMI payments
- Overdue EMIs
- EMI calendar view
- Next due date
- Monthly repayment summary

Include:
- Reminder indicators
- Payment status tracking (Paid / Due / Overdue)
- Auto-pay status indicator
- Quick pay / mark paid option

---

## 5) Prepayment Analysis
Allow users to record prepayments and show:
- Total prepayments made
- Interest saved
- Tenure reduced
- Updated outstanding balance

Include:
- Visual comparison (before vs after prepayment)
- Recommendation engine suggesting optimal prepayment timing

---

## 6) Debt Health Analysis
Calculate:
- Debt-to-Income Ratio
- Debt-to-Asset Ratio
- EMI-to-Income Ratio
- Liability contribution to net worth

Provide:
- Threshold indicators (safe / moderate / high risk)
- Recommendations when thresholds are exceeded
- Actionable suggestions such as:
  - Increase prepayments
  - Refinance loan
  - Reduce high-interest debt

---

## 7) Liability Impact on Net Worth
Integrate liabilities with assets:

Net Worth = Total Assets − Total Liabilities

Display:
- Current net worth
- Total assets
- Total liabilities
- Net worth trend over time

Include visualizations:
- Asset vs liability comparison
- Trend graph
- Debt reduction impact simulation

---

## 8) Search & Filtering
Allow filtering by:
- Loan Type

- Status (Active / Closed)
- Remaining Tenure
- Interest Rate Range

Also allow:
- Search by borrower name
- Search by loan account number
